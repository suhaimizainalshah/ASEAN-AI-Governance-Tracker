# Privacy

This page explains what the ASEAN AI Governance Tracker records about its
readers. Short version: as little as possible, and nothing about any
individual.

## What the page itself collects

Nothing. The tracker sets no cookies, runs no fingerprinting, and stores
nothing about you on any server of its own. The only things kept in your
browser are your own choices, and they never leave your device:

- Which cases or policies you follow, your light or dark choice, your
  Review-tab ticks, and the date of your last visit — all in local storage,
  in this browser only.

## Visitor counting (GoatCounter)

The live page counts visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/),
a privacy-friendly analytics service. It uses no cookies and sets nothing in
your browser.

What is collected, in aggregate only:

- How many times the page was visited, and which tabs were opened
- Which countries visits came from, and which sites referred them
- How many times the CSV files were downloaded

What is never collected or shown:

- No individual visitors. Countries and referring sites with fewer than
  3 visits are merged into "Other", and the country table appears only once
  there are at least 30 visits.
- No IP addresses, no device or browser fingerprints, no names or accounts.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected: if your browser sends
either signal, nothing is counted at all. Nothing is counted when the page is
opened from a local file. Ad blockers and privacy browsers hide a share of
visits, so the published numbers are a floor, not an exact count.

The counting code is loaded by the page itself, with the privacy checks
built in. The full snapshot is generated by `fetch_stats.py` in this
repository; it contains aggregates only.

## Third parties involved in loading this page

Only two, and no other third-party script, font, tracker or advertisement
is loaded:

- **GitHub Pages (hosting).** The page is served from GitHub Pages. When
  you load it, GitHub receives the request, as any web host does — including
  your IP address, which GitHub may keep in its server logs under its own
  privacy statement. The tracker itself does not receive or store that.
- **GoatCounter (visitor counting).** The counting script is loaded from
  `gc.zgo.at`, and counts are sent to `suhaimizs.goatcounter.com`. Those are
  the only other hosts your browser contacts to load or use this page. The
  live visitor count on the Visitors tab is fetched from the same
  GoatCounter address.

Everything else on the page is served from this site. Links out — to source
pages, to the OECD AI Incidents and Hazards Monitor, and to AI Benar
Advisory — send nothing anywhere until you click them; the destination site
then applies its own privacy policy.

## Security

The page sets Content-Security-Policy, Referrer-Policy and Permissions-Policy
through `<meta>` tags, limiting which scripts can run, how much of the page
address is shared with other sites, and switching off camera, microphone and
location features the page does not use. Two protections cannot be applied
here: GitHub Pages does not let a site set its own HTTP response headers, so
`X-Content-Type-Options` and `X-Frame-Options` (and the CSP `frame-ancestors`
rule, which also needs a header) are not set. See "Privacy and security" in
the README for the full picture.

## Data in the tracker

The tracker records public AI incidents, official government responses and
public policies, each with a source link. It contains no personal data beyond
what is already public in those sources (for example, the name of a public
official who made a statement). Summaries are paraphrases written for this
project; source material remains under its own copyright.
