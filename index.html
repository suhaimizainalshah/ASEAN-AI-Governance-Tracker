BSP Memorandum No. M-2026-031: Governance Principles for Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Financial Services

Guidance paper setting out the BSP’s minimum supervisory expectations for AI adoption by BSP-supervised financial institutions. Built around five principles — Sustainability, Transparency, Accountability, Responsibility and Security (STARS) — applied across the AI system lifecycle (plan, develop, validate, deploy, monitor) and extended to outsourced service providers under a shared-responsibility model. Voluntary and non-binding.
